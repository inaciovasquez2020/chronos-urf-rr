\par\noindent\textbf{Mathematical correction (25 November 2026).} The uniform bound on sizes of pebble-equivalence classes is false. For every $n\ge3$, $C_n$ has degree two and is vertex-transitive, so all $n$ vertices have the same pointed type even at unrestricted quantifier rank. Finiteness of the number of types never implies bounded sizes of their classes. Historical text follows; the disputed claims are not certified.\par
