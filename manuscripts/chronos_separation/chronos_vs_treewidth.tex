\section{Audit Status}

This note previously promoted several heuristic separation statements to
``theorem'' status.  The repository audit does not currently contain the
formal definitions, simulations, or independent certificates needed to retain
those statements as proved results.

In particular, the previous claims
\[
\min_\pi R^*(\mathrm{Ts}(G,f))=\Theta(\mathrm{tw}(G)),
\]
\[
R^*(F_n)=\Theta(\sqrt n),
\qquad
ED(F_n)=\Theta(n),
\]
and the assertion that fixed-$k$ Weisfeiler--Leman refinement therefore
requires $\Theta(n)$ sequential collapse are withdrawn as certified claims.

\section{Why the Previous Implications Do Not Follow Automatically}

A logical non-definability or locality statement can rule out one-shot
expression in a fixed formalism, but it does not by itself provide a
quantitative lower bound on the number of sequential refinement rounds.
Likewise, a graph-width or resolution-width lower bound does not automatically
transport to a separately defined EntropyDepth invariant.

The post-Oblivion audit has already isolated the missing structure: any such
transfer requires an explicit target process class, a formal simulation into a
proof-complexity or query model, and a quantitative inequality relating the
process cost to the certified lower bound in that model.

\begin{boundary}[Retired separation theorem]
No theorem of the form
\[
ED(F_n)=\Theta(n)
\quad\text{while}\quad
R^*(F_n)=\Theta(\sqrt n)
\]
is presently certified by this repository.  No strict EntropyDepth-versus-
treewidth or EntropyDepth-versus-resolution-rank separation is claimed here.
\end{boundary}

\section{Retained Certified Endpoints}

Two separate endpoints survive the audit.

\subsection*{Deterministic parity/query depth}
For a parity target supported on $d$ independent clause coordinates, the
Lean-certified deterministic adaptive-query model requires depth at least
$d$ for exact computation.  Under an additional round-to-query-tree
compilation hypothesis with per-round support budget $q$, this yields the
conditional bound
\[
d\le rq.
\]
This is a query-depth theorem, not an EntropyDepth or WL-round theorem.

\subsection*{Resolution width and size}
For bounded-degree expander Tseitin contradictions, the audited standard
proof-complexity endpoint is
\[
W(F_n)=\Omega(n)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Size}_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_n)=2^{\Omega(n)},
\]
using the expansion width lower bound and the Ben-Sasson--Wigderson
size--width tradeoff.  This is a theorem about general resolution
refutations, not unrestricted algorithms.

\section{Weakest Missing Objects}

To revive a quantitative treewidth/WL/EntropyDepth separation, the repository
would first need all of the following:

\begin{enumerate}
\item a formal definition of the proposed resolution-rank quantity $R^*$;
\item a proof relating $R^*$ to treewidth for the stated graph family;
\item a formal sequential WL/refinement model if round complexity is claimed;
\item a proved simulation or direct lower-bound theorem connecting that model
      to EntropyDepth or another certified cost measure.
\end{enumerate}

Until those objects exist, these are open boundaries rather than consequences
of parity undefinability or graph expansion alone.

\section{Scope}

No $\Theta(n)$ EntropyDepth theorem, no $\Theta(n)$ WL-round theorem, no
unrestricted algorithmic-time lower bound, and no P versus NP or Clay-problem
closure is claimed.
