\section{Tseitin Hard Family on Expanders}

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a connected bounded-degree graph and let
$\chi:V\to\{0,1\}$ have odd total parity.  Write
$\operatorname{Ts}(G,\chi)$ for the standard CNF encoding of the Tseitin
contradiction.  If the maximum degree is bounded by a constant $d$, then the
initial clause width is $w_0=O(d)=O(1)$ and the number of edge variables is
$|E|=\Theta(|V|)$ for a constant-degree regular family.

\section{Expansion-to-Width Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Tseitin expander resolution lower bound]
Fix constants $d$ and $\alpha>0$.  Let $(G_n)$ be a family of connected
$d$-regular graphs on $n$ vertices with linear edge expansion, and let
$\chi_n$ be any odd charge.  For
\[
F_n:=\operatorname{Ts}(G_n,\chi_n),
\]
there is a constant $c_1=c_1(d,\alpha)>0$ such that every resolution
refutation of $F_n$ has width at least
\[
W(F_n)\ge c_1 n.
\]
Consequently there is a constant $c_2=c_2(d,\alpha)>0$ such that every
resolution refutation of $F_n$ has size at least
\[
\operatorname{Size}_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_n)
\ge \exp(c_2 n).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof reduction]
The expansion argument for Tseitin contradictions gives linear resolution
width on bounded-degree expanders.  Since $w_0(F_n)=O(1)$ and the number of
variables is $|E(G_n)|=\Theta(n)$, the Ben-Sasson--Wigderson size--width
tradeoff
\[
\log \operatorname{Size}_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)
=\Omega\!\left(
\frac{(W(F)-w_0(F))^2}{N}
\right)
\]
for a CNF on $N$ variables yields
\[
\log \operatorname{Size}_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_n)=\Omega(n),
\]
and hence exponential resolution size.
\end{proof}

\section{Replacement Invariant}

For this hard family, the proof-complexity invariant is the required width gap
\[
\Delta W(F_n):=W(F_n)-w_0(F_n)=\Omega(n).
\]
This invariant directly controls general DAG-like resolution size through the
size--width theorem and does not rely on transcript multiplicity or local
FO-type diversity.

\begin{boundary}
No EntropyDepth lower bound follows from the resolution width theorem alone.
The previously stated clause
\[
ED(F_n)\ge c_3 n
\]
is withdrawn.  Likewise, no P versus NP or unrestricted algorithmic time
lower bound is claimed.  The certified scope here is resolution width and
resolution refutation size for the Tseitin expander family.
\end{boundary}
