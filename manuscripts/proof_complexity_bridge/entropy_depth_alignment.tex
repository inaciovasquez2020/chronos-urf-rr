\section{No Automatic Width--Entropy Transfer}

Let $F_n$ be a Tseitin family on constant-degree expanders.  The standard
proof-complexity results give
\[
W(F_n)=\Omega(n)
\]
and hence exponential general resolution size by the Ben-Sasson--Wigderson
size--width theorem.

These statements concern resolution refutations.  They do not, without an
additional simulation theorem, imply a lower bound for a separate
refinement-process invariant called EntropyDepth.

\begin{boundary}[Retired alignment claim]
The previously stated theorem
\[
ED(F_n)\ge c n
\]
for every ``refinement process respecting resolution locality'' is withdrawn.
The phrase ``respecting resolution locality'' did not specify a formal
simulation from such a process to a resolution refutation, nor an inequality
relating EntropyDepth to resolution width.  Therefore the width lower bound
cannot be transferred to EntropyDepth by definition alone.
\end{boundary}

\section{Weakest Missing Bridge}

Any future width-to-EntropyDepth statement must first provide explicit data:

\begin{enumerate}
\item a formal class of refinement processes $\mathcal A$;
\item a map sending every successful process $A\in\mathcal A$ on $F$ to a
      resolution refutation $\Pi_A$ of $F$;
\item a proved quantitative inequality relating the width of $\Pi_A$ to the
      claimed EntropyDepth cost of $A$.
\end{enumerate}

Only after these objects are defined and proved can a resolution width lower
bound be transported to an EntropyDepth lower bound.

\section{Current Endpoint}

For the Tseitin expander family the unconditional endpoint retained by this
bridge is
\[
W(F_n)=\Omega(n)
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\operatorname{Size}_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_n)=2^{\Omega(n)}.
\]
No EntropyDepth, unrestricted algorithmic-time, P versus NP, or Clay-problem
closure is claimed.
