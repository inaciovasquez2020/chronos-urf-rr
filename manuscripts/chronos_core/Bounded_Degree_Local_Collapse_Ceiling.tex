\section{Bounded-Degree Local Collapse Ceiling}
\label{sec:bounded_degree_collapse}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

We formalize a structural bandwidth ceiling for locality-bounded
refinement–coarsening systems on bounded-degree graphs.
This result strengthens the EntropyDepth obstruction
by isolating a purely combinatorial collapse bound.

\subsection*{Mathematical correction (26 November 2026)}
The unrestricted local entropy ceiling and its downstream collapse claims below are refuted under the stated axioms. On an uncolored cycle $C_n$, take $\tau_0$ injective and $Q_0$ the identity, then take $\tau_t$ and $Q_t$ constant with singleton color sets for every $t\ge1$. Constant local update functions satisfy local determination. Vertex transitivity in the underlying graph language gives first-order faithfulness, and the color cardinalities decrease monotonically. All color sets are realized. Thus $H_0-H_1=\log n$, unbounded at fixed degree, radius and variable count. If initial colors are instead part of the logical signature, this counterexample concerns the uncolored graph formulation actually stated; colored faithfulness would be a different hypothesis.

The replacement theorem assumes nonempty finite realized color sets and actual surjective factoring maps $f_t:D_t\to D_{t+1}$ with every fiber of size at most $B\ge1$. Then
\[
|D_t|=\sum_{d\in D_{t+1}}|f_t^{-1}(d)|\le B|D_{t+1}|,
\qquad H_0-H_m\le m\log B.
\]
The first inequality follows by counting fibers, and the second by taking logarithms and telescoping. Monotonicity of cardinalities alone does not assert such a map or bound. This replacement is sharp for a partition of $B^m$ elements into blocks of size $B$ at each step, for positive integer $B$.

For fixed degree and radius, uncolored rooted balls have at most $S=1+\sum_{j=1}^r\Delta^j$ vertices, so their isomorphism types number at most $\sum_{b=1}^S b2^{\binom b2}$. This bounds logical types of the induced balls uniformly in the number of variables. The claimed $\Theta(\Delta^r\log k)$ growth for fixed degree and radius is false as $k$ grows. Quantifier rank is not the same as graph distance, and unrestricted formulas on ambient graphs need not be determined by the induced radius-$r$ ball. Faithfulness prevents merging distinguishable states, but does not bound how many colors occupy one logical type. No unrestricted Chronos depth lower bound follows. The historical text below is retained for provenance, not as a valid theorem.

\subsection{Refinement–Coarsening Framework}

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite graph with maximum degree $\Delta$.
Let $n = |V|$.

For $r \ge 0$ define the radius-$r$ ball:
\[
\Ball_r(x) := \{ y \in V : \operatorname{dist}(x,y) \le r \}.
\]

Let
\[
\tau_t : V \to C_t
\]
be the refinement coloring at step $t$.

Let
\[
Q_t : C_t \to D_t
\]
be a coarsening operator.

\begin{definition}[Transcript Entropy]
\[
H_t := \log |D_t|.
\]
The entropy collapse after $t$ steps is
\[
H_0 - H_t.
\]
\end{definition}

\subsection{Faithful Local Coarsening (FLC)}

\begin{definition}[FLC$(\Delta,r,k)$]
The system satisfies FLC if:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Local Determination:}
There exists a function $F_t$ such that
\[
Q_{t+1}(\tau_{t+1}(x))
=
F_t\!\left(\{\!\{\tau_t(y) : y \in \Ball_r(x)\}\!\}\right).
\]

\item \textbf{$\FO^k$-Faithfulness:}
If $Q_t(\tau_t(x)) = Q_t(\tau_t(x'))$,
then $x$ and $x'$ satisfy the same $\FO^k$
formulas of quantifier rank $\le r$.

\item \textbf{Monotone Factoring:}
\[
|D_{t+1}| \le |D_t|.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

This axiomatizes the Chronos locality constraint:
collapse may forget information,
but only locally and without identifying logically distinguishable states.

\subsection{Finite Neighborhood Type Space}

By Gaifman locality,
$\FO^k$ formulas of bounded quantifier rank
are determined by bounded-radius neighborhoods.

Let
\[
\Types_{k,r,\Delta}
\]
denote the set of $\FO^k$-types of rooted
radius-$r$ balls in graphs of degree $\le \Delta$.

\begin{lemma}[Finite Type Bound]
For fixed $(\Delta,r,k)$,
\[
C(\Delta,r,k)
:=
|\Types_{k,r,\Delta}|
< \infty,
\]
and
\[
\log C(\Delta,r,k)
=
\Theta(\Delta^r \log k).
\]
\end{lemma}

This is classical finite-model theory.

\subsection{Per-Step Collapse Ceiling}

\begin{lemma}[Local Entropy Ceiling]
Under FLC$(\Delta,r,k)$,
\[
H_t - H_{t+1}
\le
\log C(\Delta,r,k).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By FLC-1, $Q_{t+1}$ depends only on
radius-$r$ neighborhood multisets.

By FLC-2, collapse can occur only within
$\FO^k$-indistinguishable neighborhood classes.

Hence $Q_{t+1}$ factors through
\[
\Ball_r(V) / {\equiv_{\FO^k}}.
\]

The number of such equivalence classes
is bounded by $C(\Delta,r,k)$.

Therefore
\[
|D_{t+1}|
\ge
\frac{|D_t|}{C(\Delta,r,k)},
\]
which implies
\[
H_t - H_{t+1}
\le
\log C(\Delta,r,k).
\]
\end{proof}

\subsection{Bounded-Degree Linear Collapse Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-Degree Linear Collapse]
\label{thm:bounded_degree_linear}
Let $G$ have maximum degree $\Delta$.
Suppose FLC$(\Delta,r,k)$ holds.
Then for all $t$:
\[
H_0 - H_t
\le
t \cdot \log C(\Delta,r,k).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\[
H_0 - H_t
=
\sum_{s=0}^{t-1}
(H_s - H_{s+1})
\le
t \log C(\Delta,r,k).
\]
\end{proof}

\subsection{Chronos Consequence}

Fix $\Delta,r$ independently of $n$.
Allow $k = k(n)$ to grow.

Then
\[
H_t - H_{t+1}
=
O(\log k(n)).
\]

In particular, if $k(n)=\Theta(\log n)$:
\[
H_t - H_{t+1}
=
O(\log\log n).
\]

\begin{corollary}[No Superlinear Collapse Under Fixed Degree]
If $\Delta$ and $r$ are fixed,
then
\[
H_0 - H_t = \omega(t)
\]
is impossible.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Structural Interpretation}

Under bounded degree and fixed locality radius,
each refinement step has finite information bandwidth.

Superlinear entropy collapse requires violating at least one of:
\begin{itemize}
\item bounded degree,
\item bounded locality radius,
\item first-order faithfulness.
\end{itemize}

Thus bounded-degree locality constitutes a rigidity wall
inside the EntropyDepth framework.
