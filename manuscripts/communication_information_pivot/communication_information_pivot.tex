\section*{Search Problem}

Let $F$ be an unsatisfiable CNF.
Define $\mathrm{Search}_F(A)$ to output a violated clause under assignment $A$.

\section*{Information Complexity}

Fix a partition $X \cup Y$ of variables.

\begin{definition}
The transcript-information quantity used in this note is
\[
IC_\mu(\mathrm{Search}_F)
=
\inf_{\Pi}
I_\mu(A ; \mathrm{Transcript}_\Pi(A)),
\]
where the infimum is taken over the protocol class specified by the intended
communication model.
\end{definition}

\section*{Raw Tseitin Boundary}

For the direct edge-variable partition of a Tseitin contradiction, Alice and
Bob can each compress their local edge assignment to a vertex-parity string.
A violated Tseitin vertex is exactly a coordinate where these two strings
differ.  Thus raw Tseitin search reduces to the Universal Relation.

Consequently, in the standard public-coin randomized communication model,
\[
IC_\mu(\mathrm{Search}_{TS(G,f)})
\le O(\log^2 n)
\]
for every input distribution $\mu$ through the standard polylogarithmic
Universal-Relation protocol.  In particular, the previously stated raw claim
\[
IC_\mu(\mathrm{Search}_{TS(G,f)})=\Omega(n)
\]
is not valid in this model.  Expansion and a balanced edge partition do not
remove this obstruction.

\section*{Surviving Candidate Route}

Strong Tseitin communication lower bounds in proof complexity are obtained
for an appropriately \emph{lifted} search problem rather than for the raw
edge partition.  A valid information-complexity route must therefore specify
an explicit gadget $g$, distribution $\mu$, and protocol model, and prove a
lower bound of the form
\[
IC_\mu(\mathrm{Search}_{TS(G,f)}\circ g)\ge L(n)
\]
inside that exact model.

Only after such a lower bound is established may it be combined with a
proof-to-communication simulation.  For general DAG-like resolution, the
currently audited simulation is space-sensitive:
\[
IC_\mu(\mathrm{Search}_F)
\le O(S(\pi)\log L(\pi)).
\]
Hence this route naturally yields a length--space tradeoff unless a stronger
proof-system-specific reduction is supplied.

\section*{Status}

The raw Tseitin linear-information claim is closed negatively.  The lifted
information-complexity route remains an explicit open target; no linear lifted
IC theorem is asserted here.  No unconditional resolution-size lower bound,
P vs NP conclusion, or Clay-problem closure follows from this pivot note.
