## 1. The previous one-variable compression is not complete

Use null coordinates 0<a=t-r<b=t+r<1. Put

    U=W_-(a,b)=(ab)^2,       V=W_+(a,b)=((1-a)(1-b))^2.

For degree ell and one fixed spherical harmonic, the radial Hilbert space is L² of the (a,b)-triangle with weight proportional to (b-a)², and multiplying by r^ell only changes this to the strictly positive interior weight r^(2ell). Let M_ell be the closed span of r^ell f(U), where f ranges over polynomials. Every member of M_ell depends on the radial coordinates through U alone after division by r^ell. This is a proper closed subspace.

Indeed the pairs (a,b)=(1/8,1/2) and (1/10,5/8) satisfy ab=1/16 yet have different (1-a)(1-b). These are interior points. More strongly, the Jacobian of (ab,(1-a)(1-b)) with respect to (a,b) is b-a>0 in the interior; hence the conditional distribution of V given U is nondegenerate on a positive-measure set. Thus V is not an L² function of U.

Let T f(y)=∫_{x<y}f(x)dμ(x) on the full diamond. The verified harmonic action with k=0, together with time reversal, gives

    T* [r^ell Y_ellm] = κ_(ell,0) r^ell Y_ellm V,

with κ_(ell,0)>0. Thus the range of T* in the ell sector is not contained in M_ell. Consequently T does not vanish on M_ell^perp and, for C_ell=T_ell* T_ell,

    b_ell - lim_d tr(P_(ell,d) C_ell P_(ell,d))
       = tr((I-P_Mell)C_ell) = ||T_ell(I-P_Mell)||_HS² > 0.

Hence the previous upper bound `s+b²-t²`, although valid for every finite d, cannot be *proved to converge to s* by merely increasing d in the W_--only test family. The earlier finite intervals remain conditional valid bounds; no claimed exact p_(4,2) follows from taking d to infinity in that restricted family.

## 2. The corrected dense basis

Both U and V take values in [0,1]. The pair (U,V) separates distinct points of the triangular null-coordinate domain: with p=sqrt(U)=ab, q=sqrt(V)=(1-a)(1-b), we recover a+b=1+p-q and a,b as the ordered roots of z²-(1+p-q)z+p=0.

Therefore the unital real algebra generated by U and V separates points of the compact closed triangle, and the Stone–Weierstrass theorem implies its polynomials are uniformly dense in continuous radial functions. Continuous functions are dense in weighted L². For fixed ell, the functions

    F_(ell,m,i,j)(a,b,n) = r^ell Y_ellm(n) U^i V^j,  i,j>=0

have dense linear span in the ell-th radial Hilbert space. The r^ell factor vanishes only on r=0, a set of zero radial measure; multiplication by r^ell has dense range. Thus the increasing orthogonal projections P_(ell,d) onto all monomials i+j<=d converge strongly to the identity on that angular sector.

## 3. Certified convergence of angular trace bounds

Let C_ell=K_ell* K_ell≥0 (trace class), b_ell=tr C_ell, and

    t_(ell,d)=tr(P C_ell P),
    s_(ell,d)=tr[(P C_ell P)^2].

For every projection P, positivity and the Hilbert–Schmidt block decomposition imply

    s_(ell,d) <= tr(C_ell²) <= s_(ell,d)+b_ell²-t_(ell,d)².

The lower endpoint increases as the projections grow, and both t_(ell,d)→b_ell and s_(ell,d)→tr(C_ell²) because P_(ell,d)→I strongly and C_ell is trace class. In particular the per-mode interval width b_ell²-t_(ell,d)² tends to zero, unlike the one-variable projection.

    ∑_(ell>L)(2ell+1) tr(C_ell²)

    p_lo(L,d) = 3557/42000 +(6/5)[A_0 + ∑_(ell=1)^L (2ell+1)s_(ell,d)],
